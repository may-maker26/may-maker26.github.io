About Me

Pursuing excellence in plasma physics research

Academic Background

I am a physics major at Miami Dade College with a deep fascination for the complex behavior of plasma systems and nonlinear dynamics. My academic journey has been shaped by a genuine curiosity about the underlying order hidden within seemingly chaotic phenomena, and a desire to develop the mathematical and computational tools to reveal it.

Through my undergraduate research with Professor Adolfo Mendez, I have built hands-on expertise in computational physics, nonlinear time-series analysis, and statistical validation.

Honors College Service Learning Reflection

What I keep thinking about when I look back at these experiences is how quickly people change when they realize something is actually theirs to figure out.

I saw this at Slingshot Marketplace. At the start, most students explained their projects in a very controlled way. It sounded prepared, but it felt distant. When I asked them simple questions like why their work mattered or who they were trying to reach, their answers shifted. Some of them paused longer. Some restarted their explanation completely. But when they spoke again, it was different. It was more direct, and people paid attention to them in a different way.

During the NASA Space Apps Challenge, teams came back tired but more certain about what they were doing. I spent most of the time checking in and helping them organize how they would present. At the end, they were not just describing outcomes. They understood their own decisions.

During the food distribution, I registered more than 675 residents, mostly seniors. One woman told me she appreciated being spoken to patiently. It was a simple comment, but it stayed with me longer than the numbers.

The same thing happened during the community cleanup and the Quinceañera event for elderly residents. The work was simple, but the conversations were not.

Writing letters and making bracelets for young cancer patients felt quieter. There was no direct response, but there was still intention behind it.

Across all of this, I started paying attention to what happens after you step back. That is the part of service that I take seriously now.
