📖 Teaching & Mentorship

Fostering collaborative learning and empowering peers in STEM

Teaching Philosophy

"I believe that effective teaching in STEM requires more than just transmitting knowledge; it involves fostering a collaborative environment where students feel empowered to ask questions, make mistakes, and discover the underlying principles of complex concepts through guided inquiry and peer-led discussion."

Miami Dade College

Responsibilities

Miami Dade College

Responsibilities

Impact
